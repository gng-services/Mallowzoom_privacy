Privacy Policy for Mallowzoom

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Mallowzoom is a children's game. We built it so that it does not collect anything about the people who play it.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best scores, which friends you have unlocked, your difficulty choice and your Settings (Slow Speed and Big Text) on your own device, so they are there the next time you play. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Internet and permissions

The app works offline. It does not ask for access to the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Third parties

The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics or social services. Google Play, the store where you download the app, has its own privacy policy that applies to your use of the store.

Children

The app is made for children aged 4 to 8 and follows the principle of collecting no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
